    Buying

    Questions collectors ask

    Where can I buy original art in Ann Arbor?

    Shop Basedment Studio online for one-of-one oil pastel and acrylic portraits made in Ann Arbor. Message @basedmentstudio to purchase or arrange local pickup.

    What mediums and sizes are available?

    Most works are oil pastel or acrylic figurative portraits in 5×8, 8×10, and select larger sizes such as 16×24. Choose unframed original, framed original, or a print in 5×8 ($40) or 8×10 ($65).

    Do you ship across Michigan and the Midwest?

    Yes. We ship originals and prints across Michigan and the Midwest. Ann Arbor and nearby collectors can also arrange pickup or local handoff by DM.

    How do I buy a piece?

    Open a work in the shop, pick original / framed / print, then use Apple Pay when available or DM @basedmentstudio with the title and option you want.

    Is this a good gift from a local Ann Arbor artist?

    Yes — originals and prints make distinctive gifts for Ann Arbor, University of Michigan, and Metro Detroit occasions: housewarmings, graduations, and first-apartment walls that need real art, not mass prints alone.
