Contemporary oil pastel portrait art

Oil pastel sits between drawing and painting: dense color you can push, scrape, and layer. Basedment Studio uses that physicality for figurative portraits — profiles, closed eyes, magenta against blue shadow — works that photograph well and hold up in person.

If you searched for oil pastel portraits for sale, oil pastel art Michigan, or a contemporary pastel artist with a modern (not traditional commission) look, this catalog is built for that search: finished one-of-ones, clear pricing, and prints when you want the image at a lower entry point.

What you can buy

  • Original oil pastel portraits in 5×8 and 8×10
  • Framed originals for immediate hanging
  • Acrylic companion pieces for collectors who want graphic color at larger scale
  • Archival-feel art prints in 5×8 ($40) and 8×10 ($65)

Who these works fit

First-apartment collectors in Ann Arbor and Detroit. Gift buyers who want a local artist, not a catalog poster. Anyone building a wall of contemporary figurative art with saturated color — magenta, window blue, warm skin against black ground.
