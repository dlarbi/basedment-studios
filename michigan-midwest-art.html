A Michigan contemporary artist you can buy from directly

Galleries in Detroit and Ann Arbor are essential — and so is a direct studio shop. Basedment Studio sells original art for sale in Michigan without a middleman markup: oil pastel and acrylic figurative portraits with clear prices, Instagram contact, and shipping built for Midwest buyers.

Cities & regions we target

  • Ann Arbor & Ypsilanti — local originals, gifts, and pickup.
  • Metro Detroit — Ferndale, Royal Oak, Midtown, and suburbs looking for contemporary portraits.
  • Michigan statewide — Grand Rapids, Lansing, Kalamazoo, and beyond via shipped parcels.
  • Broader Midwest — Chicago, Ohio, Indiana, Wisconsin collectors who want affordable figurative work.

Price range for Midwest collectors

Prints from $40 keep the door open. Small originals start around $120 unframed; framed pieces and larger acrylics scale up for statement walls. That “accessible original” band is where many Midwest first-time collectors actually buy — and where Basedment Studio is intentionally positioned.

How shipping works

After you choose a work on the shop page, message @basedmentstudio with your city and preferred option (original, framed, or print). We confirm packing, shipping cost, and timeline before payment. Apple Pay checkout is also available on-site when merchant setup is active.
